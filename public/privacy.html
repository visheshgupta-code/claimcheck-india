This notice explains what the public research prototype processes and what you should never submit.

Effective 7 September 2026 · Prototype version 0.3

Short version

ClaimCheck processes the organisation name, identifiers, claims and evidence notes that you submit so it can return an analysis. The application does not create user accounts, use advertising cookies or intentionally retain submitted case content.

Information processed

The server temporarily processes form fields included in your request. It also receives standard network information required to serve a website, such as an IP address, request time, route and browser metadata. The hosting provider may retain operational logs under its own policies.

No sensitive information

Do not submit Aadhaar or PAN numbers, bank statements, account credentials, personal financial records, confidential client information, unpublished company information or any information you do not have permission to use.

Storage, cookies and analytics

The prototype does not include a database and does not intentionally store case submissions after responding. It does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Temporary server memory and infrastructure logs may still exist while operating the service.

External regulatory links

Links to SEBI, RBI and MCA open third-party government websites. Their privacy and data-handling practices apply when you visit them.

Security and changes

The service uses request limits, security headers and HTTPS when deployed through the configured host. No internet service is risk-free. This notice may be updated as the prototype changes, and the effective date above will be revised.

Questions or removal requests

Use the contact method published alongside the portfolio project and include only the information needed to identify your request.
