Research prototype only

ClaimCheck is an educational research and triage demonstration. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with SEBI, RBI or MCA.

No advice or determination

Outputs are not legal, regulatory, compliance, accounting or investment advice. The service does not determine fraud, legality, registration status, liability, truth or investment suitability. A score is a review priority, not a verdict or approval.

Your responsibility

You must verify current rules, entity details and registration status directly with the relevant official regulator. Use qualified legal or compliance professionals before acting on an output. You are responsible for having permission to submit any content you review.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service to accuse a person or entity of wrongdoing, make automated decisions, publish unreviewed allegations, evade applicable rules, disrupt the service, test stolen data or process confidential or personal financial information.

Accuracy and availability

The deterministic rules can miss risky claims and can flag harmless language. Regulatory sources can change. The prototype is provided without a promise of accuracy, completeness, continuous availability or fitness for a particular purpose.

Changes

The service and these terms may change as the project develops. Continued use after an updated effective date means you are using the revised prototype.

Human review is mandatory

Inspect the original communication, supplied evidence, legal entity, live registry entry and current rule before reaching any conclusion.
